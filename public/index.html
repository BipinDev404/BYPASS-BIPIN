How to Install

Add the extension to Chrome, Edge, or Brave in a few clicks.

1

Download & Extract

Click the Download .ZIP button above. Once downloaded, extract the contents to a folder on your computer.

2

Open Extension Settings

In your browser, type chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions) into the URL bar and hit Enter.

3

Enable Developer Mode & Load

Toggle on Developer mode in the top right corner. Click the Load unpacked button and select the folder you extracted in step 1.

4

Pin & Setup

Click the puzzle icon in your browser toolbar, pin BYPASS Vault, and click it to perform your initial secure setup!
